A straight line from form to function

Sometimes a stroke of genius is pretty simple.

With its sleek design and reliable performance, The Stick is a kitchen tool that will revolutionize your cooking. Its round head and rodlike shape is perfect for mixing and stirring, and the long, slender handle allows for easy reach into pots and pans.

Made from 100% high-quality Swedish wood*, it's a natural and sustainable alternative to plastic or metal utensils, and will be a trusted companion in your kitchen through all phases of life.

The Stick isn't just about its natural materials and high-status appeal. What really sets The Stick apart is its deep connection to the history of human endeavors. Man lifted a branch and saw a possibility.

Holding The Stick, experimenting with new combinations of flavors and textures, you place yourself at the end of a straight line that leads back to our common roots. Man lifted a branch and saw a stick.

Possibility is in your hand.

* Available in Swedish ash, beech, birch, maple, oak, and willow.
